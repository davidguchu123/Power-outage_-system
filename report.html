Report an Outage

Fill in the form below to submit a power outage report. Your report is handled by the C++ ResidentUser::reportOutage() method.

OOP in Action: Submitting this form triggers the ResidentUser::reportOutage() override (Polymorphism + Inheritance). The OutageReport object is constructed using Encapsulation — all fields are private with setter methods.

What Happens After You Submit?

🔒
Encapsulation

Your data is stored via private setters on the OutageReport class.

🧬
Polymorphism

The backend calls reportOutage() on either a ResidentUser or AdminUser depending on your role.

💾
Persistence

Reports are serialized to reports.dat using file handling (no STL).

📱
SMS Alert

A simulated SMS is triggered if the area has a HIGH outage frequency.
